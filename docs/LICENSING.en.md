The project is released under the [GNU General Public License v3.0](https://www.gnu.org/licenses/gpl-3.0.html) — free software with copyleft. Anyone may:

- **use** the firmware for any purpose, including commercial;
- **study** the source and modify it for their own needs;
- **redistribute** the original and their modifications, including for a fee.

The one copyleft condition: derivative works must stay under GPL-3.0, and their source code must be made available to recipients. This keeps the freedom you receive flowing to every downstream user.
